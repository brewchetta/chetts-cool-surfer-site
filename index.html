About Me

Dude, welcome! I'm Chett, and the ocean is basically my second home.

Whenever the swell is pumping, you'll find me out past the break, chasing that one perfect ride. Wax on the board, salt in the hair, sun on my face. That's the dream, and I'm living it.

When the waves go flat, I'm still on the move: skating, riding snow, and finding the next adventure. Life's too short for boring days.
