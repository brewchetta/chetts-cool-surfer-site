Home Turf

Mordor

The break where it all started, and where I'm still out at sunrise.

One does not simply paddle into Mordor. The waves are molten, the sky is on fire and the lineup is mostly Orcs, but the barrels are Mount Doom-sized. Best surfed at low tide, when the lava cools and the Eye is looking the other way.

  • Wave type: Molten lava barrels
  • Best swell: Ash from Mount Doom
  • Best season: Endless winter of the Dark Lord
